🌍 COP31 • ANTALYA • 9–20 NOV 2026

Yetim Vakfı — SWOT Analysis & Green Zone Acceptance Strategy

Can the foundation get accepted to the COP31 Green Zone? Where is it strong, where is it weak — and what 10 moves will maximize its acceptance chance? Prepared 25 September 2026.

⚖️ Verdict in 30 seconds

YES, acceptance is realistic — but only in the Climate Supporter / NGO lane of the Green Zone, NOT the Startup Zone (that lane is only for climate-tech startups). The winning formula: apply now + show one real climate-linked project + frame it as "children & climate justice". A pure orphan-aid application with no climate link will likely be rejected.

🔍 SWOT Analysis

💪 S — Strengths (internal)

  • Big, trusted Turkish NGO (founded 2017) — host-country advantage: Türkiye wants strong local civil society at its COP.
  • Proven giant scale: 3.6M+ people reached in 38 countries (2025); Ramadan operations in 30 countries.
  • Livelihood projects = hidden climate gold: vegetable farming (Bangladesh), dairy/livestock (Somalia, Yemen), weaving & sewing cooperatives — honestly frameable as climate adaptation & family resilience.
  • Strong digital power: donation app (iOS + Android) + online platform — perfect for an interactive booth demo.
  • Youth, volunteers & emotional stories — children-focused messaging the media loves.
  • Corporate donor base — can finance booth, travel and materials.

🩹 W — Weaknesses (internal)

  • Not a climate organization — core mission is orphans/humanitarian; weak climate track record vs. green NGOs.
  • No flagship "green" project — no solar, reforestation, water or clean-energy program to exhibit yet.
  • Startup Zone doesn't fit — must compete in the crowded NGO lane.
  • Time is very short — ~6 weeks to 9 Nov; late applications risk "no space left".
  • No climate-framed materials yet — website and docs are 100% orphan-focused.
  • Greenwashing risk — climate words without a real project will look fake to reviewers.

🚀 O — Opportunities (external)

  • Green Zone explicitly welcomes NGOs, youth & civil society — not only tech companies.
  • Perfect theme fit: climate justice, children & climate, adaptation, loss & damage, youth.
  • COP-ART & COP-ENTERTAINMENT programs: children's art from recycled materials = ideal entry ticket.
  • Joint pavilions: partner with a university, green NGO or corporate for a stronger application.
  • Launch moment: announcing a NEW climate project at COP31 is itself a strong argument.
  • Fundraising & visibility: global donors, media and corporates in one place for 12 days.

⛈️ T — Threats (external)

  • High competition — many NGOs applying; limited Green Zone space.
  • Strict relevance filter — weak climate connection = rejection.
  • Greenwashing scrutiny from press and activists.
  • Real costs: booth, team in Antalya for 12 days, materials, side events.
  • Political sensitivity: Gaza/humanitarian messaging at a UN event must stay diplomatic.
  • Process confusion: Green Zone, Startup Zone, Partnership portal, visitor QR = 4 separate tracks.

⚡ 10 Power Moves to Maximize Acceptance

1
🔴 DO NOW

Apply in the RIGHT lane

Email greenzone@cop31.tr + sponsorship@cop31.tr as "Climate Supporter". Never apply as a startup — instant mismatch.

2
🔴 DO NOW

Launch ONE real climate-linked project

E.g. "Solar power for 10 orphanages" or "1,000 climate-resilient family farms". One tangible project beats 100 posters — and kills the greenwashing risk.

3
🔴 DO NOW

Own the narrative: children & climate justice

"Children on the Frontlines of the Climate Crisis" — orphans of floods, droughts and displacement. Data + real stories.

4
🟡 THIS WEEK

Apply WITH a partner

Joint booth with a university, environmental NGO or corporate sponsor = a much stronger file for reviewers.

5
🟡 THIS WEEK

Enter via COP-ART

Exhibit children's art made from recycled materials. Emotional, on-theme, cheap — and hard to reject.

6
🟡 OCTOBER

Propose a side-event panel

"Protecting Children in a Warming World" — with speakers from partners. Panels multiply visibility 10×.

7
🟡 OCTOBER

Build an interactive booth

Live app demo, QR donations, pledge wall, impact counter. Reviewers love exhibits visitors can touch.

8
🟡 OCTOBER

Attach a media plan

Press kit + social campaign + Turkish & intl media list. Shows you bring attention to COP31, not just take space.

9
🔴 DO NOW

Send a professional file

1-page concept note + budget + team + visuals, in English (plus Turkish). Amateur files get ignored.

10
🟢 AT COP

Register the team early

All booth staff need visitor registration + QR via cop31.tr/register-to-visit. Don't leave it to November.

🗓️ 6-Week Action Timeline

  • 📩 Week 1 (now – 2 Oct): concept note + application emails + partner outreach + pick the climate project to launch.
  • 🤝 Week 2–3 (3 – 16 Oct): confirm partner, design project + booth concept, propose side event & COP-ART entry.
  • 🎨 Week 4 (17 – 23 Oct): booth design, print materials, app-demo station, press kit, team visitor registration.
  • ✈️ Week 5–6 (24 Oct – 8 Nov): logistics (Antalya hotels, transport, shifts), rehearsal, social countdown.
  • 🌍 9 – 20 Nov: COP31! (Note: 11–12 Nov are World Leaders Summit days — check the official program for visitor access.)
